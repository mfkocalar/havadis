## Phase Goal

**As a** security expert reading Havadis on a phone or a desktop, **I want to** see the day's articles laid out as a newspaper grid under urgency-ordered sections, with per-section counts and the snapshot's freshness, and narrow it to the sections I care about with one tap, **so that** I can triage what matters without reloading the page or scrolling past sections I don't need.

(Same story as 04-01, framed from the ROADMAP Phase 4 Goal line and Success Criteria.)

<objective>
Complete the front page's metadata and length handling. After this plan the reader:
- sees when the cached snapshot was built, as an honest, ticking "Updated N m ago" in the sticky bar;
- sees each long section trimmed to its top 6 cards behind a client-side "Show all N".

This plan owns the "last updated" half of UI-04 and the section-length half of UI-01. It implements D-05, D-06, D-07, D-12 and D-13.

Purpose: Task 1 is this plan's tracer, and the only slice in the phase that touches the pipeline layer. It threads a snapshot timestamp from `getFrontPage` through the server page into a client leaf, and proves it in the production HTML. Task 2 refines that slice into the hydration-safe relative clock. Task 3 adds the cap and expander slice into the grid and the `CARD_GRID_CLASSES` constant that 04-01 created.
Output: `generatedAt` on the ok result, the UTC formatters, `formatRelativeTime`'s optional `now`, `LastUpdated`, the cap constants, `planSectionCap`, `SectionExpander`, and production-build invariants.

D-13 honesty decision (resolves the CONTEXT caveat):
- The relative age is computed on the client from `generatedAt`, so it reports the age of the snapshot the reader is actually looking at, and it keeps ticking.
- Server HTML and the hydration pass carry the deterministic `Updated HH:MM UTC`, so a cached page is never misleading and hydration never mismatches.
- `generatedAt` is the page snapshot's build time. Feed bytes may come from the per-fetch Data Cache and be up to one 900 s window older (RESEARCH Pitfall 4b, Assumption A2). That is accepted; it is checked against the newest article time on the preview in 04-03 Task 3.
</objective>
