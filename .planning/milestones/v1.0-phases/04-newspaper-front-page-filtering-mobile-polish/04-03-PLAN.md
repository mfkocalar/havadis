## Phase Goal

**As a** security expert reading Havadis on a phone or a desktop, **I want to** see the day's articles laid out as a newspaper grid under urgency-ordered sections, with per-section counts and the snapshot's freshness, and narrow it to the sections I care about with one tap, **so that** I can triage what matters without reloading the page or scrolling past sections I don't need.

(Same story as 04-01, framed from the ROADMAP Phase 4 Goal line and Success Criteria.)

<objective>
Prove that the finished page meets UI-05 at real device widths, as ROADMAP Success Criterion 3 requires ("verified at real device widths, not just a resized desktop browser window"). Also prove FILTER-01's "no new server fetch" in a real browser. This plan runs the UI-SPEC Verification Contract's tier 2 and tier 3:
- Tier 2: a reproducible Chromium emulation run over six widths against the production build. It checks overflow, columns, the pill scroller, tap targets, the sticky bar, focus offset, zero-request interactions, hydration and console cleanliness.
- Tier 3: the user's own check on a real iOS phone and a real Android phone against a Vercel preview.

Tier 1 (hermetic and production-HTML tests) already ran in 04-01 and 04-02.

Why this plan has no tracer: it adds no capability, and its only artifact is evidence. Its first runnable automation needs Playwright. The package-legitimacy protocol requires a blocking-human checkpoint before that install (04-RESEARCH.md flags `playwright` as SUS on the recency heuristic). So Task 1 must be that checkpoint. The phase's architectural tracers are 04-01 Task 1 and 04-02 Task 1.

Sign-offs collected at Task 3:
- The flagged UI-SPEC amendment from 04-01: `ring-zinc-500` on unpressed pills and the expander, instead of `ring-zinc-400` / `dark:ring-zinc-600`.

Output: `scripts/verify-viewports.mjs` and a preview deployment URL. Recorded evidence goes in 04-03-SUMMARY.md: per-width results, the expander-exercised flag, the title-clamp rates, the screenshot directory, the preview URL with its HTTP status, the real devices used, and both sign-off decisions.

- `time[data-last-updated]`: `Updated HH:MM UTC` in server HTML, relative after hydration.
