the code: one deliberately broken source among healthy ones must not stop the rest, a rejected
fetcher must be swallowed identically to an error-variant result, and N sources must be in
flight simultaneously.

the phase's headline guarantee. ROADMAP Success Criterion 2 is written as a deliberate-breakage
test, and Success Criterion 1 says the parallelism must be "observable as concurrent, not
sequential" — both need a hermetic harness, because deliberately breaking a real external source
is not possible.
Output: three new fixture routes and two test files that pin failure isolation, timeout
isolation, the rejected-settlement branch, and wall-clock concurrency.
