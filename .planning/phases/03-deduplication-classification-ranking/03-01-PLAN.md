<objective>
Turn Phase 2's flat, recency-sorted list into a sectioned, ranked front page: every article is
classified into exactly one of the 7 sections by tuned keyword rules (title first, then summary,
default Industry/Policy), sections render in operational-urgency order, and articles are ranked
inside each section by tier weight x recency decay. This plan owns CLASSIFY-01, CLASSIFY-02 and
CLASSIFY-03 and implements D-05 through D-12 and D-16.

**As a** security expert, **I want to** open the front page and see today's stories sorted into
urgency-ordered sections with the most important current item at the top of each, **so that** I
triage the vulnerabilities and advisories first without scanning one long undifferentiated list.

Purpose: the tracer proves the new pipeline shape (classify -> group -> sectioned render) end to
end on the real page before ranking and the classification fixtures are layered on; dedupe
(Plan 03-02) and CVE chips (Plan 03-03) then slot into the `composeFrontPage` seam this plan
creates without reshaping it.
Output: `Section`/`ClassifiedArticle`/`SectionGroup`/`SectionedFrontPageResult` types, the
`sections.ts` and `ranking.ts` config modules, `classify`, `groupBySection` and `rank` stages,
a `composeFrontPage` seam, the stacked-section `page.tsx`, and the fixture + live + real-page tests.
</objective>
